**Your magical kingdom is under attack**, and you are hungry as a horse with a horn! Defend your home and satiate your endless hunger in this score-attack game!

**The goal** is to get these invaders out of your hair, while racking up a high score. Running into an enemy will get you points, and stick the enemy onto your conveniently built-in grill spear. Taking a full spear to a grill grants a temporary score multiplier, depending on how many different kinds of enemies are on your spear.

**You control** the direction of the unicorn using the mouse, jump using the spacebar and dash using the shift key.
